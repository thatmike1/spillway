A candidate missing every new experiment could therefore pass these catalog gates. This is a coverage hole, not evidence the current candidate omitted them: `look/evidence/r19-final-contracts/PACKAGING-FREEZE.json` names Tether Yard, Fall Line and Line Drive in addition to the pre-existing lab, and Saltglass as the new surf map.

Small fix for the parent: make freeze validation require three to five unique experimental map IDs drawn from the new maps, excluding the old `experiment` ID; retain the old lab separately, and add refusal tests for zero, two and six new experiments plus acceptance of three and five. The release controls should also explicitly verify the experiments are presented under the Esc menu's Experiments section, since the current probe checks map selection and title, not the section placement. Do not change the frozen candidate underneath an active package; any helper change needs a new committed source freeze and fresh package evidence.
