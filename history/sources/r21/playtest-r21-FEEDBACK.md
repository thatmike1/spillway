# Mike's R21 playtest, 12 Nov 2026

His recordings since the R20 batch are in userdata-spillway/r16_runs/ (8 saltglass, 7 tether-yard, 1 breakwater; each file names its build). His two screenshots are in screenshots/.

Saltglass:
- The lower exits do carry his speed. That fix worked.
- The surf ramps are one flat solid colour, so their shape is hard to read, and they blend into the dark undersides of the platforms (screenshots/ramp-blends-with-platform-underside.png).
- There are boxes floating in the air with no support (screenshots/floating-boxes.png). He asked whether they are meant to be like that.
- He could not find the fast outside route near the quay at the end.
- It does not feel full, only less barren. In his words, it feels like the agent thought "throw more shapes into the map to make it seem like I filled it with life".

Tether Yard:
- Needs more testing, but right away Arc (the slower-reel Elastic) feels best.
- It made him want to experiment a lot more with swinging maps. He felt a bit like Spider-Man, and that could be fun.

He did not raise Through Line, the backwards stack or the anchor readout this time.
