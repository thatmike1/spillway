Date: 2026-11-06 (source commit author date, Europe/Prague offset in Git: 2026-11-06T08:39:22+01:00). Build: `spillway-2026.11.06-r11`. Runtime source: `52a66297bafb6bd401b0c519c2a1ef0f9e7a9cc6`. Engine: `4.7.2.stable.official.ed1daf0bf`.

Unless expressly attributed as recorded words, every paragraph below is an **agent summary** of the cited sources. R1–R11 are numbered Spillway deliveries following the Foldrunner reboot, not the older reconstruction milestones. See ../../TIMELINE.md for the complete chronology.

## What was asked

Continue the meaningful game work overnight through at least 08:00 Prague on 6 November, preserving R10 as a stable checkpoint. After delivery, Mike played R11 and requested the bhop/surf direction that became R12.

## What was built

Backward braking became at least as firm as release. Late-duck eye clearance and audio loops were repaired; reachable hardware gained collision, the finish gained guards and front arrival, and the terrace was screened to lead toward the yard. A working bucketed wheel, gate, flume, tailrace and concrete pours improved the finish. The basin ramp was cleared and widened, and batching preserved screen shadow settings.

## Mike’s verdict and recorded words

Agent-recorded summary, not a verbatim quote: SURF-BRIEF.md says Mike found plain running boring, sliding fun and accidental bunny hopping the best part. Turning while hopping lost speed; the left stair route became a long walk. It records his wish to lean into Counter-Strike-style bhop/surf. playtest-r11/README.md records wheel snagging, the non-solid gate beam, water stalling slides, a floating rail and the reservoir edge. These documents attribute the feedback to Mike, but do not preserve a verbatim message. No invented direct quote is supplied.

## Key decisions

Keep forgiving recovery while disclosing its weak Outflow risk tradeoff. Isolate export tests so loose source cannot hide missing packed resources. Resume after disk exhaustion without claiming uninterrupted overnight work. Preserve limitations: two follower stalls, no independent full R11 discovery finish, and no proof of enjoyment from automated checks.
