# Plan Yard presentation

The sky and mathematical wear are existing original project assets documented in `assets/look/PROVENANCE.md`. The yard shaders are original project code.

The concrete shader also reuses the unmodified White Plaster Rough 02 diffuse texture already shipped in the R15 Breakwater kit, by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0-1.0. Its source, licence and hash are recorded in `assets/r15/breakwater/MANIFEST.json` and `licences/CC0-1.0-legalcode.txt` there. The shader desaturates it to luminance, applies restrained variation to teal concrete and repeats it at 3 m. No new downloaded asset or private reference is imported.
