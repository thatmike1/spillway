# R15 selected asset library

Selected models and associated textures by Kenney are distributed under CC0 1.0. Original per-pack notices, including additional creator credits where present, remain in `licences/`.

Snow 02 by Rob Tuytel, from Poly Haven, is also CC0 1.0. The original 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and ARM files are retained unchanged, with publisher MD5 verification recorded in MANIFEST.json.

MANIFEST.json records exact source archives, selected members, source pages, modifications and file hashes. BOUNDS.json records unscaled imported dimensions/material-surface counts and a suggested placement correction. Course instance scales and collision proxies are separate authored choices.

These are candidate source assets for the five R15 worlds. Inclusion here does not establish that a model is used by a completed map, that its collision is valid, or that its final lighting/materials are visually accepted. No publisher preview images or complete pack exports are included.
