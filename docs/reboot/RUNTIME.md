# Spillway runtime

The active contract is ../surf/BRIEF.md. This directory's BRIEF.md supplies the dam setting, art direction and three stages. Its old air-speed restrictions are superseded. The default scene uses spillway_main.gd. Earlier Mirror's Edge controllers/worlds remain historical fixtures outside the portable pack.

spillway_player.gd owns the CharacterBody3D, collision and velocity. Metres, seconds and radians, 120 Hz physics. Its origin is at the feet, with standing and crouched capsule heights. Standing clearance is checked before growth. Visible stair treads do not collide; continuous ramps carry the capsule. Camera interpolation, a restrained landing dip and optional gait motion leave free look and body motion alone. New values are P choices.

Air acceleration uses the velocity projection along the wish direction and a 0.75 m/s wish cap. A strafe key with a matching mouse turn can gain speed; looking without input cannot change airborne velocity. Held or buffered jump fires before ground friction. Autohop defaults on and is a pause setting. Horizontal authoritative velocity is capped at30 m/s. Surfaces above46° and through75° can be surf contacts. Real face normals distinguish a ramp from rounded capsule edge contacts. Contact-cone resolution preserves clear tangent motion at creases; a high-energy pre-sweep prevents a collision from moving the body above the cap. Safe-margin recovery adds a small measured displacement allowance, recorded in the motor evidence.

Downhill slides gain speed up to16 m/s without clipping faster carried speed. Wet slides add0.25 m/s² drag; ordinary wading stays slow. Repeated crouch presses cannot supply speed. Opposing ground input brakes before reversing. A fresh intentional mantle is limited to10 m/s, cancellable and swept. Holding jump through an autohop landing cannot silently catch a lip until jump is released.

spillway_level.gd builds Crest, the shared materials, lighting, landscape, water and original machinery. spillway_course.gd builds the yard and Outflow. spillway_surf_geometry.gd creates curved banks with matching render and convex collision vertices. Fixed valve wheels use smooth convex discs beneath their visible spokes. Reachable pipe, beam and roof solids have collision. Rails stand on supported decks and exposed shallow water has retaining faces. Static boxes batch by material and requested shadow setting, while physics bodies remain separate. The live finish wheel stays outside static batches.

spillway_main.gd owns input, run provenance, pause/settings, checkpoints, recovery and records. R resets the attempt; F and out-of-bounds respawn invalidate clean eligibility. Ordered full-course records and splits require all stages in sequence. Arrival at the wheel always finishes. A clean skipped-stage arrival can set a separate open-line best; it receives no invented split or ordered record. Bot and headless runs never persist. R12 uses user://spillway_r12.cfg and records_r12; R11 settings seed a first start, while R11 records remain separate and its file is never rewritten. The pause menu identifies the build.

spillway_audio.gd uses original synthesis from audio.gd for material footfalls, impacts, wind, slide/surf contact and spatial water. Wind describes speed through30 m/s. Surf contact scrape fades on departure; water softens slide scrape. Signal and mixer checks establish behavior, not listening quality. Audio streams stop and release before exit. Running arms are intentionally absent.

spillway_finish_presentation.gd supplies the pump-hall facade, bucketed wheel, gate, flume and tailrace. Wheel arrival opens the gate and starts water; restart resets it. These thin visual skins add no collision. The moving wheel remains inside its physical guard.

The ordinary waypoint driver and the surf driver inject action/mouse events. Neither owns the player trajectory or run state. The surf driver follows a state-aware line rather than a human recording; its success establishes one executable line. The --crest slice and --no-batch rendering comparison remain developer options. Verification boundaries and delivered results are separate documents.

The native package uses an explicit resource list and isolated preflight, followed by packed startup and route checks. BUILD.json and checksums identify the source and official pinned Godot4.7.2 runtimes. The tools-enabled runtime is larger than a stripped template. Native Windows gameplay remains unverified on the Linux host. Current development readiness is in ../surf/STATUS.md; this runtime description is not a delivery certificate.
