# F4: Quake III-inspired projection

P comparison, 8 November 2026. F4 is an extra movement law beside the requested Source
baseline, not a replacement for it and not a Quake or CPMA recreation. The default stays F2
(CS:GO bhop server). F4 offers Quake III's broader projection: the full requested speed is
the air ceiling, where Source caps the air projection at 30 u.

## What it is

F4 is one entry in `scripts/r14_presets.gd` (`quake3_inspired128`, aliases `quake`, `f4`).
It is a configuration of the same original projection module (`r14_source_motion.gd`) and
the same repaired Source collision adapter (`r14_source_player.gd`, family version
`r14-source-motion-3`, including the later contact guard). No motor was cloned and no engine code was copied. Switching between
F2 and F4 keeps the same body; the main loop treats it like any other Source preset.

| Setting | F4 | Where it comes from |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| ground acceleration | 10 | Q3 `pm_accelerate` (WIDE.md) |
| air acceleration | 1 | Q3 `pm_airaccelerate` |
| friction / stopspeed | 6 / 100 | Q3 `pm_friction`, `pm_stopspeed` |
| wishspeed, cap, player and server max | 320 u/s | Q3 `g_speed` 320; the air ceiling is the full wish |
| gravity / jump impulse | 800 / 270 | Q3 `g_gravity`, `JUMP_VELOCITY` |
| jump order | ideal (exact ballistic) | P: the closest of the module's two orders to a 270 u/s launch |
| air surface | constant 1 | no Source deadstrafe window |
| stamina, takeoff clamp, ground clamp | off | not part of Q3's walk or air move |

Comparison choices, labelled P: 128 Hz ticks, autohop while Space is held (released Q3 needs
a fresh press), the Source 32 × 72 u box and 64 u eye (Q3's hull is 30 u wide in `bg_pmove.c`; its
height and eye constants are in files not downloaded), 1 u = 0.0254 m, the shared crouch scale 0.34 (Q3: 0.25), Source's per-axis
`sv_maxvelocity` 3500 bound and Source's mouse units (sensitivity × 0.022 degrees per count).

What it lacks, so it is only Quake III-inspired: Q3's command subdivision (a usercmd split
into moves of at most 66 ms, or `pmove_msec`), the velocity snap to whole units after every
move, Q3's own vertical integration in its slide move (not downloaded), the `cmdscale` term that shrinks the wish while jump is held in the air, Q3's
1.001 overclip and its own slide, step and ground-trace contacts, ramp and slope rules, and
any CPM air control. No CPM option is included.

## Controls

The HUD's always-visible line reads `F4 Quake III-inspired · 128 Hz · AA 1 ·
320 u wish, not 30 u · not Quake`, short enough to stay clear of the practice tag. The intro text
and the pause menu give F4's own controls: WASD runs at 320 u/s, hold Space to keep hopping
(the wheel works too). In the air, hold W with A or D and turn the mouse gently the same way.
Letting go of every key coasts on the current line. The keys line names `F4 Q3-inspired`.

The strafe HUD keeps its A/D definitions: W+A counts as an A tick for sync. Gain, contact and
ground loss mean the same as for the Source presets.

## Measured differences

All through actual key, wheel and relative-mouse events in the R14 main on the labelled
loop fixture, unless marked. Speeds are horizontal, in u/s.

- Best one-tick air gain at 320 u/s, 128 Hz (closed form): F4 2.480620 at a wish 7.17°
  off travel; F2 1.4032 near 90°. At 45° off travel F4 still gains 1.77 a tick, F2 nothing.
- The independent 64-case closed-form file (`quake-prototype/oracle.json`, not generated by
  this game) agrees with F4's tick to at most 4.8e-7 u/s.
- Ground run with W: F4 320.0, F2 250.0. W, A, W+A and W+D all wish exactly 320.
- One tapped hop on flat ground: apex 45.56 u (the 270/800 ballistic peak; F2 55.83 u).
- One hop from each law's own run speed, view turning left 0.004 rad a tick:

| Input | F4 (320 at takeoff) | F2 (249.6 at takeoff) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| W+A, left turn | +134.7 | 0.0 |
| A, left turn | +32.1 | +12.7 |
| W+A, no mouse | +71.9 (the path curves) | 0.0 |
| no keys, mouse turning | 0.0 | 0.0 |

These are fixed imperfect inputs, not optimal arcs, and they start at different speeds.

## Switching and records

The general rules in PLAYABLE.md apply unchanged. Measured for F4: mid-air F2 → F4 → F3 →
F4 → F2 keeps feet, velocity, yaw and pitch exactly each time (a new body only for F3);
a started or early-hop attempt becomes practice; a switch at the untouched spawn stays clean;
the first hop straight after switching is counted; F1 (64 Hz) → F4 settles to 1/128 with
one stale callback logged. F4 has its own record key. On the fixture a clean F4 run sets
F4's best (4.625 s against F2's 5.953 s), a mixed F2 → F4 attempt and a stage practice
finish unrecorded, and a fresh game on the same profile loads only F4's own best. The record
section stores the full F4 configuration, including `sv_air_max_wishspeed` 320.

## Overflow route and native renders

The following is the earlier F4 integration evidence. Its geometry, times and family-2
contacts are historical; current family-3 packed acceptance is indexed in RESULTS.md.

A fresh input-only route, recorded for F4 and not a retimed Source tape: the old closed-loop
ordinary driver ran inside the actual R14 main on the unchanged Overflow geometry
(`3a04610b…`), its 0.0022 rad/px constant rescaled from the runner's own Source radians per
count. It recorded key edges and relative mouse only, at F4's 128 Hz. The open-loop replay of
that tape through `tools/momentum-tape.py` with `--motor quake` finishes clean in 35.67 s
with no respawn and three jumps (F2's root tape on the same geometry: 45.82 s). The native
render (640 × 360, llvmpipe on Xvfb) matches the headless replay on all 4961 ticks, and a
native render of F2's unchanged root tape matches root's headless run on all 6248 ticks.

A native tour in the actual main on Penstock Yard (geometry `506ce7f9…`, the checkout's
practice map, which is under separate revision) switches F2 to F4 mid-air with an exact pose,
then runs five F4 hops (250 to 796 u/s, no contact), a no-key coast hop (gain 0) and a
grounded switch back to F2, whose fixed W+A hop gains 0. Its F4 hops come from a closed-loop
driver that holds the wish 2° past the full-projection limit, so they show what the law
allows, not what a player does.

These are scripted lines on two geometries, not evidence that F4 suits a course, feels right
or performs on native Windows or a GPU. Evidence is outside Git in
`checkpoints/spillway/r14/quake-product/RESULTS.json`.
