# Spillway r21

Spillway is a first-person momentum game about earning speed, hopping between platforms and finding cleaner lines through checkpoint courses.

R21 keeps the approved F2 bhop movement and original courses. Saltglass has lower surf exits and an outside line around the quay. Tether Yard adds Soft, Firm and Arc rope variants, optional anchor acquisition assistance and revised transfer decks. Through Line and the Movement Lab remain separate experiments. The pale decks, dark sides, painted skies and L look comparison are included.

Choose the Linux or Windows ZIP, extract it and use its included launcher. These are the exact verified delivery ZIPs, preserved without rebuilding during the repository handover. They retain build identity `spillway-2026.11.11-r21` and original source `dee210165b6f169b2433b3043712c5855c37d3b6`. The repository's readable source layout has separate parity verification.

Use the attached SHA256SUMS to verify either download. Controls and source orientation are in START-HERE.md on the repository's main branch.
