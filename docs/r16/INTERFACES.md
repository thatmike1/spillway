# R16 component contract

Read BRIEF.md first. R16 composes new services around the unchanged R15 course runner and maps. Root owns `r16_main.gd`, its launch/scene/default wiring, `r16_hud.gd`, R16 profile/record identity, optional map overlay and final delivery. Components below have their own new files and tests. Preserve all R15/R14 runtime files.

## Common post-step sample

Root samples after the actual motor, once per physics step. Services receive a Dictionary with: `tick:int` (attempt-local, starts0), `dt:float`, `physics_hz:int`, `elapsed:float` (course clock), `started:bool`, `finished:bool`, `clean:bool`, `practice_reason:String`, `checkpoint:int`, `respawns:int`, `p:Vector3` (actual feet), `reference_p:Vector3` (feet excluding outstanding duck-origin compensation), `v:Vector3` (stored m/s), `actual_v:Vector3` (finite-difference travel excluding legitimate hull compensation), `yaw:float`, `pitch:float`, `grounded:bool`, `ducked:bool`, `motion:Dictionary` (`player.get_motion_state()`), `stats:Dictionary` (`r14_strafe_stats.snapshot()`), `held:Dictionary` (action booleans), `input_events:Array` (ordered key/button edges and relative mouse deltas since last sample). Explicit jump/fall/respawn/settings/pause/map/preset/loadout boundaries are lifecycle events; a discontinuity must not look like a giant gap or speed gain. No service writes position, velocity, grounding, inputs or progress.

Root will provide helper construction and ordering. Standalone component tests can build this same sample after a labelled fixture setup. If a mandatory field needs changing, document it here/in your handoff before integration; do not silently use another meaning. Vectors in recording serialization become numeric arrays; full dictionaries may be sparse context/events rather than repeated every tick.

## Style: `scripts/r16_style.gd`

Prefer `extends RefCounted`. Public API: `reset(context:Dictionary={})`, `step(sample:Dictionary, body:CharacterBody3D, level:Node3D)->Array` (new scored events), `discontinuity(reason:String)` (clear flight/probe continuity; preserve or reset run total explicitly), `snapshot()->Dictionary`, and a constant `STYLE_VERSION`. Snapshot includes integer `score`, combo/multiplier, recent event and detector diagnostics. Scored events are serializable Dictionaries with attempt tick/sim time, stable kind, points/base/multiplier and measurements sufficient for analysis. Geometry identities use stable paths/face information rather than process-local instance ids in the recording.

Use real space queries/hull/contact geometry for edge/gap/clearance/crouch-saved. A standing counterfactual is the appropriate standing hull swept at the same motion/centre as the actual crouched hull, with support floors and starts-overlapping cases distinguished. Pay out for actual passage, not merely a nearby obstacle or duck press. Sync needs eligible turning samples and positive real gain, not directional sync alone. State exactly what each detector proves and where it is approximate.

Own optional `scripts/r16_style_panel.gd`: a compact additive Control/Canvas child with `attach(main,style_service)`, frame update and no gameplay writes. Root wires it through R16 HUD. Preserve old identity/speed/strafe/control/menu hooks. Native inspection must include event bursts and finish/menu states at normal resolutions; a headless Label string is not visual acceptance.

## Recordings/analysis/ghost

Own `scripts/r16_run_recorder.gd`, `scripts/r16_run_reader.gd` and `scripts/r16_ghost.gd`, `tools/analyze-r16-runs.py`, dedicated `tests/r16_record*` / `test_r16_record*` and `docs/r16/RECORDINGS.md`.

Recorder API: `begin_run(metadata:Dictionary,directory:String)->Dictionary`, `capture(sample:Dictionary,events:Array=[])->void`, `event(kind:String,data:Dictionary={})`, `finish(outcome:Dictionary)->Dictionary` (ok,path,run_id,counts/status), `abort(reason:String)`, `is_open()->bool`. Bounded buffering, atomic completion and explicitly recognizable incomplete/cancelled/disk-error states. Every played attempt starts at spawn/restart/practice; R/F, quit, map and preset changes finalize the old attempt appropriately. Falls/respawns are events within practice, not clean bests. Finish seals the run at the line; untimed exploration afterward need not pretend to be another finished attempt. Idle/pause/timer-start semantics must be documented. Root records actual inputs from event delivery and the post-step sample; recorder never generates game input.

Default path: `user://r16_runs/`. Player scene enables recording. Programmatic `new()`/test runs are off unless explicitly enabled into isolated directories; root will expose `--r16-recordings=DIR` and `--no-run-recordings`. `--no-records` refers to best/config persistence and is distinct from recording files. Format must be versioned, compact, append/stream bounded and independently readable with Python's standard library. `FileAccess.open_compressed` is Godot-specific, not an interchangeable gzip file. Verify actual format APIs and cross-reader bytes. Include map/preset/physics/geometry/scoring identities and header plus per-tick context change semantics, precise positions/views and discrete edges, lifecycle/style/outcome. No arbitrary-code or resource loading from copied recordings.

Reader supports strict complete validation and explicitly labelled partial recovery. Folder analysis reports speed loss with context, falls/restarts, spatial line summaries, style totals/events and outcome per run; do not confuse duck-origin shifts or respawns with earned speed/gaps. Corrupt/unsupported files are reported without masking valid neighbors. Document units and errors. A recording is observational evidence, not a promise that engine input replay is deterministic on another machine.

Ghost API: `load_run(path:String,expected_identity:Dictionary)->Dictionary`, `set_time(elapsed_s:float)`, `clear()`, and `visible` toggle. Nonphysical Node3D; interpolation over recorded positions/view, timer-start alignment, identity validation and no old-course/preset ghost. Root owns choosing/promoting the fastest clean complete recording path in R16 config; service can supply helpers. Ghost may use a simple original translucent body/trail; preserve landing visibility and hide near the player's eye. No floor/progress collision or runtime network dependency.

## Lab: `scripts/r16_experiment_player.gd`

Extend frozen `r15_experiment_player.gd` and override only the affected wall/slide hooks/tick ordering needed. Root selects it solely for map kind lab. Keep inherited `r15_slide` (Shift), `r15_grapple` (E), anchor groups and grapple routines unchanged. Course presets still use exact `r15_source_player.gd`; no Source-law edits. Expose `r16_lab_version` or `movement_version()` for identity, updated mechanics getter/settings that includes actual new wall/slide tuning while retaining the exact old grapple table, and detailed events/diagnostics in `get_motion_state().experiment`.

Own lab-specific standalone fixture driver/tests/docs and optional R16 lab art/helper, not main/HUD/presets/maps or frozen lab geometry. Root updates the lab controls and signs after the law is proven. Pick a bounded wall policy that cannot reset repeatedly on stale support or oscillating between two faces. It must work while A/D remains held immediately after a strafe hop; don't ask the player to switch to W. Preserve tangent travel where appropriate and distinguish a deliberate push window from permanent air-law changes.

Shift is the starting slide-input choice: buffer/arm during descent to catch the landing, preserve entry speed, take precedence over held autohop for that landing, and allow a deliberate fresh hop out. Exact buffering/exit rule is yours to choose and label, provided ordinary Ctrl/C crouch-hop remains unchanged. Test both held-Space entry and manual entry, false ground/stale support, roofs/edges, reset/toggle and wall/slide combinations. Prove unchanged grapple inputs and disabled/no-move parity with R15.
