Gate and the early container sequence were attempted from the real default spawn. Box rows activated visibly. This is a bounded synthetic-input discoverability result, not a human feel verdict, movement acceptance, packed acceptance, or a claim that a human cannot finish.

## What the views establish

- The default is visibly BREAKWATER, F2 CS:GO bhop server, 128 Hz, AA 100. The gate points down a broad straight corridor. WASD, held Space, same-direction air strafe, crouch, R, F and Esc are explained on screen.
- Yellow roof edges, visible landing roofs, and 2.0 m / 79 u, 2.5 m / 98 u and 3.0 m / 118 u markings identify the container jump sequence. Their near-edge geometry is harder to judge from the default level view: three real misses ended against container sides. Those misses are observed outcomes under this discontinuous input harness, not proof of defective movement or impossible gaps.
- Repeated container colours do little to distinguish progress. The transient Box rows popup is the clearest confirmation of advancement. Its on-screen hint explicitly names a right-side recovery ramp. The right-side floor alley was found, but the ramp was not identified in the eight-decision search after miss 3. This does not establish that the ramp is absent.
- F works as visible recovery: after miss 1 it returned to Gate, and after miss 2 to the earned Box rows checkpoint. Both displays clearly say practice and not recorded. The floor-level recovery search after miss 3 did not achieve a physical return to roof height.
- Crane line's visible instruction gives a route sequence: left bend, weave two stacks, right onto the breakwater. Open pavement around the stacks is visible. The first turn was taken too early and caused a wall collision; the route was subsequently followed around the stacks. The curved causeway was missed into water, and the HUD explicitly showed an automatic return to Crane line practice.
- The breakwater straight has the strongest long-range destination cue: a lighthouse with a moving beam. The crown-wall distance marks and yellow water edge make direction and progress clear. The final striped gap and far landing become obvious near the edge. The last gap, The breach checkpoint and the finish were not tested.
- The menu clearly names all five practice positions and says checkpoint practice is not recorded. One popup click failed to change selection; one Down/Return sequence selected Gate rather than Crane line. Three Down events from the popup's initial keyboard state selected Crane line, and four selected Breakwater. These are counted navigation errors, not evidence of lost gameplay controls.
