### 3. Missing considerations and a sharper decision question

- **Define the failure before prescribing a motor:** distinguish input responsiveness, acceleration/steering, landing and collision consistency, camera, scale, readability, route design and speed feedback. State which are evidenced complaints and which are candidate explanations. Include novice recoverability, skill expression and enjoyable mistakes; perfect traversal can conceal all three.
- **Separate two decisions:** what platform cheaply resolves the next uncertainty, and what platform best supports the eventual product. Define the product requirements that could justify migration independently of feel, including actual target platforms, performance, iteration burden, licensing and maintainability. Existing exports alone establish none of their priority.
- **Prevent confounded comparisons:** identify what must stay comparable across slices and what may change. Changing engine, motor, course, scale and presentation together could yield a preferred build without revealing why. Do not require identical internals where that would unfairly handicap a candidate.
- **Specify an evidence ladder:** arithmetic checks model behavior; fixtures check invariants/regressions; imperfect inputs test robustness; visual agents test navigability under their actual observation limits. None establishes human handling quality. Record remaining uncertainty explicitly and preserve a later human comparison without making Mike's live attendance a blocker today.
- **Set symmetric decision rules:** state what would overturn both “keep Godot” and “migrate,” as well as the favored steering diagnosis. Require each experiment to retire a named uncertainty; do not count sunk work, novelty, higher bot completion or a larger framework as a win by themselves.
- **Sharper question:** “Which unresolved causes of R12's rejected handling can we distinguish with the smallest playable comparisons, what evidence would support or overturn each explanation, and which engine decision can that evidence justify now versus leave open?” This permits autonomous progress without pretending the brief already defines satisfying feel.
