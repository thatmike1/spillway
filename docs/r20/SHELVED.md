# Shelved experiments

Line Drive and Fall Line are parked following Mike’s R19 playtest. They are absent from R20 map selection; their source stays available for future work.

## Line Drive

The pads read as flat lines without useful feedback, the landing target is unclear, and Mike’s own built speed beat what the pads offered. Revisit it only with an unmistakable launch shape, feedback at contact and launch, a visible landing target, and a useful result for a fast incoming run.

The recordings contain eight attempts, seven restarts and one clean finish. They support the high incoming speed; they do not record pad support or launch impulses, so they cannot establish why an individual pad did or did not fire.

## Fall Line

Contact and collapse gave Mike no perceptible sound or visual feedback. The two-second timer is too long at bhop speed: he could hop two or three times on a block without it falling beneath him. A revival needs an immediate readable response and a theme that explains the collapse, such as lily pads on water or scaffolding.

The three recordings include a stationary fall consistent with the timer working. Source also contains countdown and drop visuals. Neither fact overturns the playtest feedback: those cues did not communicate the mechanism to Mike.
